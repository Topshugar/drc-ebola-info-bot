> Offline-first life-saving USSD/SMS bot for eastern Congo. Works on any dumb phone, no internet needed.

### Crisis Now (Oct 2026)
- **4,018+ dead, 8,300+ cases** - worst DRC outbreak ever (Bundibugyo strain)
- **Kigonze Transit Centre burned Oct 2**, 19,000 displaced
- Ituri epicenter = 81% of cases

### What it does
Dial `*XXX#` and get:
1. Symptoms - what to look for
2. Prevention - how to stay safe
3. Centres Ouverts - live OPEN/CLOSED status
4. Urgence 109 - free emergency number

Languages: **Français, Swahili, Lingala**
